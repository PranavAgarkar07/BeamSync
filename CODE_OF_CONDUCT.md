# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- **Be respectful**: Treat others with courtesy and respect.
- **Be collaborative**: Encourage constructive feedback and collaboration.
- **Avoid harassment**: No harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- **Stay on topic**: Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of harassment or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `maintainers@beamsync.org`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and appropriate actions will be taken.
